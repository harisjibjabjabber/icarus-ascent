## Paused (placeholder, planned after the character lands)
Style target for the world: Studio Ghibli × Zelda: Breath of the Wild × soft anime — painterly
skies with big sculpted cumulus, a soft volumetric-looking cloud sea, floating islands with grass
and cypress swaying in wind, warm late-summer light, marble/bronze/terracotta with soft painted
textures. Workstreams to plan later: Environment & sky & clouds · Rendering pipeline & post
(bloom, grade LUT, aerial perspective, AA, quality tiers kept: `?q=low|medium|high` + auto
fallback) · VFX & juice (speed lines, grind sparks, landing dust, pillar shatter, FOV kicks).
Gameplay readability rules (rails dark bronze vs pale marble, pots/pillars warm-lit, gaps with
edge markers) stay non-negotiable. Seam refactor + worktree split deliberately deferred.
