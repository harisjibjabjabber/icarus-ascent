# Star trail placement

Collectibles are short trails of dainty stars. Placement is a level-design pass, not a density quota.

## Purpose

Every gold trail teaches or rewards one line:

- **Jump arc** — ballistic samples of a real jump (`jumpVelocity` 9.2, air gravity 30) across a jump gap, so the stars draw the hop.
- **Rail** — evenly timed along a required or optional grind (never the risky high line; that line is purple).
- **Wall ride** — a flat line at 42% of the wall height, 0.35 m off the face, so holding the ride collects it. Short taps carry 4 stars; longer rides up to 9. The line stops before 78% when the wall is long or required, leaving the end clear.
- **Pot hop** — one jump over a compulsory pot row, clear of the props.
- **Pot smash** — a smooth side-step through a pot row when the hop does not fit.
- **Ramp approach** — a grounded lead-in on kickers, crests, and set pieces.
- **Rail breadcrumb** — a short street line that bends toward an optional rail too brief to carry its own grind.
- **Safe street / weave** — a grounded centre-line, or one gentle sine past props. Long breathers may carry one; connectors stay empty.

The highest-purpose line that starts after the breather wins. A long phrase may carry a second line only after the first has ended and the breather has elapsed. Two lines never overlap.

## Motion

Stars are spaced about **0.3 s** apart at the phrase cruise speed (5–8 stars, about 1.5–2.2 s of riding). The shape is a single smooth move: a parabola, a rail, or a straight street. No lateral zig-zags.

## Rhythm

After a trail ends, the next gold trail waits **30–44 m**. That breather keeps a single line ahead. A long phrase may carry a second line only after the wait, and only when the rest of the phrase can hold it.

## Purple

A single purple star marks a mastery point about **every 250 m** (cursor **160–210 m** after the last one, taken at the next real point, or the best mastery in a phrase that has already gone overdue): the far end of a risky rail, **88% along a long or required wall** at the 42% ride height, the apex of a jump, or the end of a readable rail. The opening high line still carries its purple on the risky rail. It does not sit on a gold star.

## Presentation

Gold stars are about 0.44 m (roughly 8–10 px at mid-distance, capped near 18 px beside Ao), with a slow spin and an out-of-phase twinkle. Purple is 1.5× that, a saturated violet with a soft halo and a stronger twinkle. Pickup is a small star-pop, about 200 ms. Counts and placement are unchanged.
